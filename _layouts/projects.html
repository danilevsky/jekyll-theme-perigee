Cards are laid out as a "bento" gallery (see /styleguide/), gravity-ui.com's "Our libraries" style: one large featured tile plus smaller ones. The first project with `featured: true` in its front matter is used as the large tile; with none flagged, the first project in the collection is used. It's rendered first in the grid — with dense packing, a later large tile tends to leave a gap where an earlier auto-placed small tile already claimed part of its 2x2 area.
